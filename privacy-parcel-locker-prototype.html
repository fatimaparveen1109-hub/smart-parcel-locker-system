How it works

One order moves through every dashboard and all of them read the same record.

  1. Order

    Customer picks products and two preferred hubs.

  2. Pack

    Seller packs and hands over to a rider.

  3. Deliver

    Rider chooses a hub, a compatible locker is allocated.

  4. Pay

    Pay now, or after the locker deposit.

  5. Collect

    Pickup QR unlocks only after payment is verified.
